Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about curating and ordering your wholesale jewellery kit.

What is included in the Wholesale Jewelry Sample Kit?
Each kit includes authentic 92.5 Sterling Silver jewellery pieces hand-picked by you, authentic certified purity, protective pouches, and transparent cost breakdowns.
How does the factory making charge pricing work?
You get direct factory transparency: you pay introductory wholesale making charges (starting from ₹135/g to ₹155/g depending on kit tier), with zero retail markups, that too with ultra low MOQ.
Can I keep the sample pieces or sell them in my store?
Yes, 100%. All sample kit pieces are completely yours upon delivery. You can display them in your boutique showcase, test customer demand, or retail them immediately.
How many pieces can I choose, and can I mix categories?
You can choose Mini Kit (5 pieces), Classic Kit (10 pieces), or Mega Kit (20 pieces). You have total freedom to mix rings, pendants, earrings, bracelets, and chains across the entire catalog.
How fast is dispatch and what are delivery charges?
Kits are dispatched via express insured courier within 24 to 48 hours of order placement. Mega kits include 100% free delivery across India.
What payment options are available?
We support instant 1-click UPI via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM, dynamic on-screen QR code scanning from another phone, and credit/debit cards via secure SMEPay gateway.
